    What is Hackshelf?

    Hackshelf is a deadline tracker for hackathons. Paste the link, add the deadline, and it joins one list sorted by what closes first.

    It replaces the screenshots in your gallery and the saved messages you never open again.

    • Screenshots buried in your gallery
    • WhatsApp messages to yourself
    • That Notion page you stopped opening
    • One shelf, sorted by deadline.

    How to use it

    1. 1

      Save it

      Paste the link when you find a hackathon. Add the deadline and you're done.

    2. 2

      Track it

      Move it from interested to registered, team formed, submitted and result.

    3. 3

      Stay ahead

      Color-coded urgency counters highlight what closes today, this week, and later.

    Features

    • One shelf

      Devfolio, Unstop, Devpost, MLH and anything else, sorted by deadline.

    • Status pipeline

      Know which ones you registered for and which still need a team.

    • Team notes

      Keep teammates and the group chat link next to the hackathon.

    • Fast & private

      Works offline instantly. Zero login barriers, your data stays in your browser.
