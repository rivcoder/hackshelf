Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Local-First Philosophy

Hackshelf is designed with a local-first approach. In v1, all your tracked hackathons, deadlines, notes, tags, and team chat links are stored exclusively inside your browser's local storage (localStorage). No hackathon data is sent to or stored on our servers.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect:

  • Your name, email address, or contact details.
  • Your browsing history or third-party cookies.
  • Unannounced telemetry, advertising tracking, or analytics.

3. Your Data, Your Control

You own your data completely:

  • Export Anytime: Use the Export button in the app to download a clean JSON copy of all your saved hackathons.
  • Import / Transfer: Move your saved data to any device or browser with the Import button.
  • Delete Instantly: Deleting an item or clearing your browser cookies permanently removes it from your device.

4. Future Cloud Sync (v2)

When cloud synchronization and Google Sign-In are introduced in v2, they will be entirely optional. If you choose to sign in, your hackathons will be protected with Row Level Security (RLS) so that only your authenticated account can ever read or update your rows.

5. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or Hackshelf, you can reach out via GitHub: github.com/rivcoder/hackshelf.
